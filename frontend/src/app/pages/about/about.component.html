About ParkSmart

Our mission is simple: no driver should waste time hunting for parking, and no parking space should sit empty and earning nothing.

Why we exist

Parking wastes everyone's time

ParkSmart started with a familiar frustration: circling crowded blocks, guessing which garage still has room, and arriving late because of it. Drivers lose hours every month just searching for a place to leave their car — time that adds stress, fuel costs, and congestion to already busy cities.

At the same time, countless garages, lots, and private spaces sit half-empty for most of the day. Their owners have no easy way to reach the drivers who need them. We built ParkSmart to connect these two sides in one simple marketplace.

Today, drivers search live availability, compare prices, and book in seconds — while owners list their spaces, manage bookings, and earn steady income from square meters that used to sit idle. That is the future of parking we are building.
