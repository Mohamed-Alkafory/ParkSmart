FAQ

Common questions

How do I book a parking spot?

Create an account, search for parking near your destination, pick an available spot, and confirm your booking. You will get an instant confirmation with all details.

How do I list my parking space?

Register as an owner, add your parking with its location, price, and spots, then manage bookings and earnings from your owner dashboard.

Is ParkSmart free to use?

Creating an account and browsing parkings is free. Drivers only pay the hourly price shown for the spot they book — no hidden fees.

Can I cancel a booking?

Yes. Open My Bookings, choose the booking, and cancel it. The spot immediately becomes available for other drivers again.
